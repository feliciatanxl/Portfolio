ShieldQuest

Project SHIELD | Cooperative 3D Crime-Prevention Adventure

cd ../projects

Project Overview

Project SHIELD: ShieldQuest is a youth-led, cooperative 3D crime-prevention game developed by Team SecurePi for Delta Challenge 2026 — Track B: Crime Prevention. The challenge involves the Singapore Police Force (SPF) and National Crime Prevention Council (NCPC); ShieldQuest is a team project, not an official SPF or NCPC product.

Built around the idea “Experience the consequence. Understand the pressure. Protect someone else.”, ShieldQuest aims to turn crime-prevention awareness into interactive decision-making. The refined initial pilot focuses on shop-theft prevention and the peer pressure and safe bystander responses surrounding it.

The Problem

Knowing what is right does not always make it easy to respond safely under pressure. ShieldQuest gives youths a fictional, low-risk environment to practise recognising clues, refusing involvement and supporting others before encountering similar choices in real life.

The Proposed Game Experience

Players will control their own avatars in a shared low-poly 3D retail environment, explore with a private squad of up to five players, investigate cases, complete mini-games and decide how to respond. A board-inspired mission system will guide progression, while a Delayed Consequence Engine will show how choices can affect later events.

>_ Core USP

Explore together. Decide under pressure. Experience consequences that can return later in the story.

Key Game Systems

3D World Exploration

Move freely through Singapore-inspired retail spaces and interact with fictional characters and clues.

Five-Player Squads

Join friends or be grouped into private teams, with each player controlling an avatar.

Cases & Mini-Games

Investigate shop-theft situations through clue hunts, dialogue challenges and cooperative puzzles.

Peer Shield Decisions

Use private voting, group discussion and a shared final decision through Think–Vote–Explain.

Delayed Consequences

See immediate outcomes and later story events shaped by earlier decisions.

Guardians & Progression

Earn game XP and Guardian progress while keeping rewards separate from learning assessments.

How a Workshop Mission Would Work

  1. 01 / Join — Scan a facilitator's QR code and join or create a squad of up to five.
  2. 02 / Explore — Enter a shared 3D retail mission and investigate clues together.
  3. 03 / Play — Complete skill-based mini-games and respond privately to key decisions.
  4. 04 / Discuss — Compare anonymous votes and agree on a team response.
  5. 05 / Experience — Discover immediate and delayed consequences of the choice.
  6. 06 / Reflect — Complete individual SHIELD assessments and review learning feedback.

For the proposed school and community pilot, facilitators would host sessions, manage squad allocation and view aggregate learning results. These multiplayer and facilitator workflows are part of the new build, not features claimed to be complete in the earlier prototypes.

The SHIELD Learning Framework

The six proposed competencies are designed to assess different behaviours: Spot factual clues, Hold back from unsafe action, Identify risk and influence, Evaluate response options, Lead through safe peer communication and Defend by seeking appropriate help. Each would use its own interaction and scoring criteria.

Individual pre- and post-game tasks are planned to measure learning improvements separately from team mission results, game points and Guardian rewards. No pilot results are claimed at this stage.

Milestone & Development Status

Team SecurePi presented ShieldQuest at NYC's Young ChangeMakers Open Mic at Impact IRL on 31 October 2026. In November 2026, the team received conditional grant approval, subject to clarifying gameplay, narrowing the crime-prevention focus and defining measurable SHIELD outcomes. Full approval remains pending.

Earlier prototypes explored the game's interface and 3D movement. The project is now being rebuilt in a new repository around a more focused shop-theft pilot, proposed cooperative multiplayer missions and learning evaluation. The broader ambition is a game that Singaporeans could eventually play beyond facilitated community sessions.
