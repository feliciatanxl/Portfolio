ShieldQuest

Project SHIELD | Interactive Crime Prevention

cd ../projects

Project Overview

Project SHIELD: ShieldQuest is a youth-led, interactive crime-prevention experience created for Delta Challenge 2026: Track B — Crime Prevention, a challenge associated with the Singapore Police Force (SPF) and National Crime Prevention Council (NCPC).

Designed as a mobile-first Progressive Web Application (PWA), ShieldQuest turns passive awareness into decision-making practice. Players navigate realistic situations involving scams, suspicious requests, peer pressure, and everyday safety risks, then observe the immediate and delayed consequences of their choices.

The Problem

Knowing about crime risks does not always translate into safer actions when someone faces pressure, uncertainty, or a convincing real-world scenario. Conventional awareness material can explain warning signs, but may not give young people an opportunity to practise making decisions in context.

ShieldQuest explores whether interactive scenarios, peer-oriented learning and feedback on consequences can bridge that awareness-to-action gap.

Our Approach

The initial rollout focuses on tertiary students, with the intention to progressively expand engagement to other youth audiences. The experience combines scenario-based storytelling, player choices, consequence feedback, and progression mechanics.

>_ Design principle

Build an experience where players do more than identify a risk: they practise a decision, discover its consequences and reflect on how they might act outside the game.

Core Prototype Features

Interactive City-Board

A navigable experience connecting different crime-prevention scenarios within a game-like environment.

Delayed Impact Engine

Choices can influence outcomes later in the experience, encouraging players to think beyond immediate rewards.

Peer SHIELD Mode

Explores the role of peers and intervention when navigating risky situations.

Guardian Progression

Player progression designed to encourage continued engagement with learning scenarios.

Scenario Management

Supports the organisation and development of interactive safety and crime-prevention scenarios.

Mobile-First PWA

Responsive web delivery intended to make the experience accessible from everyday devices.

How the Experience Works

  1. 01 / Explore — Navigate the city-board and encounter a scenario.
  2. 02 / Decide — Select a response to a realistic crime-prevention challenge.
  3. 03 / Experience — Observe immediate feedback and potential delayed consequences.
  4. 04 / Reflect — Consider safer decision-making and peer intervention.
  5. 05 / Progress — Continue through scenarios and Guardian progression.

The SHIELD Framework & Behavioural Impact

The SHIELD Framework is intended to support structured behavioural learning. During the NYC Young ChangeMakers Open Mic, SPF representatives encouraged the team to review possible overlaps between framework components and ensure that each component has its own observable, measurable outcome.

The next design consideration is to define distinct behavioural indicators, compare responses before and after activities, and use follow-up evaluation to understand whether learning translates into better decisions. These measures are proposed evaluation directions, not completed study findings.

NYC YCM Open Mic Pitch

On 31 October 2026, Team SecurePi pitched ShieldQuest at the National Youth Council (NYC) Young ChangeMakers (YCM) Open Mic, requesting S$5,000 in funding for a proposed six-month implementation phase.

The discussion included SPF feedback on strengthening SHIELD's behavioural measurement and NYC conversations about outreach support and budget allocation. The team explained how potential outreach assistance could allow more of the proposed budget to support game assets and hosting, including performance and latency considerations.

>_ Grant status: Outcome pending

The Open Mic pitch is completed. The S$5,000 amount reflects the team's funding request, not an awarded grant.

Development & Iteration

The project includes an initial proof-of-concept and subsequent ShieldQuest prototype iterations. Multiple repositories and deployments document the team's exploration of gameplay mechanics, the player experience and application delivery.

Work is ongoing across scenario design, UI/UX, interactive mechanics, implementation, and preparation for future piloting. Features and deployment environments may differ between versions.

My Project Experience

Through Team SecurePi's development and pitching process, the project has involved translating a social-impact problem into a technical concept, shaping scenario-based interactions, evaluating practical delivery considerations, and presenting the proposed solution to external stakeholders.

The Open Mic discussion also highlighted the importance of making behavioural outcomes independently measurable, connecting game design decisions to evaluation methods, and explaining how development resources would support a realistic implementation plan.
