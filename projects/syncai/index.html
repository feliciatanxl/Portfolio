Project Overview

SyncAI is an AI-powered Smart Work Planner concept developed by a three-member team for EMS Challenge 12i. It explores how Town Council officers can prioritise their responsibilities, organise available time and respond more effectively to unexpected changes.

Designed as a Microsoft-based workforce planning assistant, SyncAI aims to bring together assigned tasks, calendar commitments and practical AI recommendations in a single planning experience for officers and supervisors.

This page documents ongoing project discovery, architecture planning and prototype exploration. The AI features and Microsoft 365 integrations below describe proposed capabilities unless explicitly stated otherwise.

The Problem We Are Exploring

Officers juggle deadlines, meetings, recurring duties and urgent operational requests. A task list alone cannot tell them whether everything can realistically fit into their day or what needs to change when priorities shift.

Supervisors may also need better visibility into team capacity, potential delays and operational continuity when an officer becomes unexpectedly unavailable. These are design questions to validate with the client, not claims of measured problems at a particular Town Council.

What We're Designing

> AI Task Intelligence: Explore natural-language task input, explainable priority recommendations, effort estimates and deadline-aware task organisation.

> Smart Daily & Weekly Planning: Generate feasible suggested time blocks around meetings, working hours, task effort and dependencies.

> Dynamic Replanning: Demonstrate how urgent assignments affect the current schedule, with proposed alternatives for officer approval.

> Team Capacity & Continuity: Explore workload visibility, early risk indicators, unexpected absence scenarios and supervisor-reviewed reassignment suggestions.

01 / UI & Prototype Exploration

The initial direction is a responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript concept prototype with separate officer and supervisor experiences. The interface is being explored around a personal dashboard, Smart Planner, workload overview, AI insights and a What-If Simulator.

02 / My Role & Contributions

Working with two teammates, I am involved in exploring the challenge requirements and shaping how SyncAI's AI modules, user journeys and Microsoft-based architecture could fit together. Responsibilities will continue to evolve as the team's implementation scope is finalised.

> Requirements & Problem Discovery: Develop meaningful questions around prioritisation, scheduling, staff availability, workload capacity and supervisory decision-making.

> AI Feature Exploration: Brainstorm task intelligence, feasible schedule generation, workload forecasting and absence impact simulations.

> Frontend & UX Prototyping: Explore a clear, responsive officer dashboard and supervisor interface before moving to the full application.

> Technical Planning: Evaluate React, Node.js/Express, PostgreSQL, Microsoft Graph and Azure AI services, including JavaScript versus TypeScript options.

03 / Proposed Microsoft & AI Architecture

SyncAI is planned around a web frontend, an API backend and a shared data model. Subject to the client's environment and permissions, Microsoft Entra ID could provide authentication, while Microsoft Graph could connect calendars and tasks from Outlook and Microsoft To Do.

> Microsoft Data: Outlook Calendar and Microsoft To Do as the initial integration priorities; Teams, Planner and SharePoint remain optional.

> Task Understanding: Azure OpenAI is being considered for extracting tasks and explaining recommendations, with application-side validation.

> Scheduling Logic: A rule-based or constraint-aware engine would check availability and feasibility rather than allowing a language model to invent a timetable.

> Human Oversight: Officers and supervisors would review proposed scheduling or reassignment changes before anything is written back.

04 / Development Approach

> Discovery: Validate real operational workflows, constraints, user roles and success criteria with EMS.

> Prototype: Build interactive officer and supervisor screens using synthetic tasks, calendars and disruption scenarios.

> MVP: Develop task management, feasible scheduling and clear explanations with a shared application data model.

> Integration & Evaluation: Add approved Microsoft APIs and AI services where accessible, then evaluate planning accuracy, usefulness and usability.

Project status: Early-stage discovery and prototype exploration. Proposed AI intelligence, performance gains and external integrations are not presented as completed, validated or deployed functionality. This independent portfolio summary does not imply EMS endorsement.
