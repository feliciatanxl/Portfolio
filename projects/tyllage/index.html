Project Overview

Tyllage is a Singapore-registered AgriTech software venture developing practical, farm-first tools to improve sales operations, customer engagement, demand visibility and commercial decision-making.

Our current focus is an AI-assisted farm sales and operations platform that connects upcoming harvest batches, customer enquiries, pre-orders, marketing campaigns and buyer relationships. The aim is to help farms identify sales opportunities earlier and reduce avoidable administrative work and unsold produce.

This page is part of my personal engineering portfolio and documents my work as Tyllage's founder. Tyllage is an LLP with two founding partners; its proprietary company work is not my personal property merely because it appears in this portfolio.

The Problem We Are Exploring

Farm sales workflows can be fragmented across online stores, spreadsheets, messages and manual follow-ups. Farms may have limited visibility into how upcoming harvest supply compares with confirmed demand, while campaigns and buyer outreach take time to coordinate.

These are product hypotheses being investigated through discovery and prototype testing; they are not presented as verified operational findings or quantified results for MEOD Farm.

What We're Building

> Harvest & Demand Visibility: Manually record expected harvest batches, compare them with confirmed orders and pre-orders, and show uncovered quantities without requiring IoT sensors.

> AI Campaign Advisor: Help farm staff prepare sales outreach and campaign suggestions from approved product, stock and demand information, subject to human review.

> Customer & Buyer Engagement: Explore WhatsApp-assisted enquiry handling, buyer follow-ups, multilingual communication and escalation to farm staff.

> Learning Journey Enquiries: Support farm-visit and educational programme enquiries, follow-ups and potential commercial opportunities.

02 / My Role & Contributions

As the founder of Tyllage, I initiated the venture and work with my fellow founding partner and team on its product direction, software development and operational planning.

> Product & Solution Design: Define the farm-first problem scope, feature priorities, workflows and validation approach.

> Full-Stack Development: Contribute to React/TypeScript interfaces, Node.js/Express application architecture, data-model planning and API integration approaches.

> Cloud & Security Planning: Evaluate AWS deployment, access controls, logging, secret handling and reliable environments for future pilots.

> Team & Business Coordination: Work jointly with the other founding partner on company direction, financial oversight, team onboarding and project delivery under the LLP's agreed governance arrangements.

03 / Development Approach

> Current: Demonstrate and refine data-light farm workflows with synthetic data and structured user feedback.

> Next: Validate farm requirements, harvest-batch records, demand-gap indicators, pre-order flows and staff-approved campaign recommendations.

> Pilot readiness: Seek permission for limited integration with existing farm systems; test authentication, privacy, security and operational procedures before handling live data.

> Future exploration: Consider more advanced forecasting and commercial simulations only when sufficient reliable data and validation are available. IoT and robotics are outside the current MVP.
