Project Overview

Tyllage is an emerging AgriTech platform exploring how software, connected sensors, and AI-assisted workflows can support farm operations and business growth.

Its development currently spans three distinct initiatives: an IoT MVP originally intended for MEOD Farm, a ComCrop solution prototype, and a separate MEOD Farm sales AI automation initiative in progress.

The Problem

Farm operations and commercial processes can become fragmented when environmental readings, crop information, customer enquiries, and sales follow-ups are managed through separate tools or manual workflows.

Tyllage explores practical approaches to two different needs: improving visibility into farm conditions and supporting the commercial side of selling farm produce.

What Tyllage Covers

> IoT MVP: Environmental sensing, device-to-dashboard data flows, and early crop intelligence experiments. Developed as a standalone prototype, originally intended for MEOD Farm.

> ComCrop Prototype: A publicly accessible prototype for proposed digital farm workflows, with product planning, deployment feasibility, and commercial considerations under exploration.

> MEOD Farm Sales AI Automation: An independent work-in-progress initiative focused on customer enquiries, WhatsApp-assisted communication, sales follow-ups, and produce order workflows.

01 / IoT MVP Prototype

The IoT MVP investigates how environmental sensor readings can be surfaced through a digital interface. My work has included ESP32-based experimentation and exploring the connection between physical hardware, application services, and farm-facing dashboards.

Although originally intended for MEOD Farm, this prototype is presented separately from MEOD's planned sales automation work.

02 / ComCrop Prototype

The ComCrop track translates farm workflow ideas into a demonstrable application and a proposed implementation scope. It considers how an integrated platform might support monitoring, operational visibility, and commercial planning.

The prototype is publicly accessible on Railway. The broader work remains at the prototype and proposal stage rather than a confirmed farm deployment.

03 / MEOD Farm Sales AI Automation

This is a separate business-focused initiative, not a rename of the IoT MVP. The intended direction is an AI-assisted sales workflow that can help manage incoming produce enquiries, WhatsApp communication, follow-ups, and potential order handoffs.

> Current status: Work in progress. No public sales automation demo is available yet.

> Planned workflow: Customer enquiry → AI-assisted response / qualification → follow-up → human review or order handling.

My Contributions

My work across Tyllage combines product scoping, frontend and backend prototyping, IoT experiments, interface design, solution research, deployment exploration, and commercial feasibility planning.

The project also gives me experience thinking beyond individual software features: connecting technical feasibility with operational needs, implementation cost, and business value.

Development Roadmap

> IoT: Refine sensor-data ingestion, dashboard presentation, and prototype validation.

> ComCrop: Improve the solution prototype and validate requirements against the proposal.

> MEOD Sales AI: Develop a demonstrable customer enquiry and follow-up workflow before claiming an operational integration.
