SoLeaf

AI-Powered Farm Sales Automation

cd ../projects

The Problem

For farms selling to repeat buyers, a simple enquiry can trigger several manual steps: checking stock, confirming quantities or pricing, updating an order record and following up with the customer. When those details are spread across chats and spreadsheets, fulfilling the next order can be harder than it should be.

These are the problems informing our product discovery, not verified claims about any named farm's operations.

Our Product

Our immediate focus is AI-assisted WhatsApp sales automation backed by farm-controlled information. Instead of a generic chatbot that only responds to messages, the proposed system helps connect each enquiry to the farm's inventory and sales workflow.

  1. 01 / INBOXCustomer enquiryBuyer messages the farm
  2. 02 / AIAssisted replyUnderstand the request
  3. 03 / CHECKVerify detailsStock and buyer pricing
  4. 04 / ORDERConfirm or escalateHuman review when needed
  5. 05 / OPSFarm dashboardTrack orders and fulfilment

Proposed MVP workflow. AI and WhatsApp Business API integrations are planned; they are not described here as production-ready.

What Makes It Farm-Aware?

> Stock-backed conversations: Design replies around information approved by the farm rather than invented availability or pricing.

> Orders, not just chats: Connect enquiries to structured order records and a farm-operator view.

> Human oversight: Route uncertain requests, exceptions and sensitive decisions to staff for review.

> Repeat-buyer relationships: Make it easier for farms to support familiar buyers and future reorders as the product matures.

02 / My Role & Contributions

As a co-founder of SoLeaf, I work with my co-founder and team to turn farm-sales challenges into a focused product roadmap, prototypes and implementation plans. My work sits across software engineering and technical product management.

> Product Strategy: Translate feedback into MVP priorities, user stories, requirements, workflows and pilot-validation criteria.

> Software Engineering: Work on full-stack prototype development and plan the React, Node.js/Express, API and PostgreSQL architecture.

> AI & Integration Design: Define stock-aware responses, order handoff and human escalation; investigate the WhatsApp Business API and model integration.

> Team & Delivery: Coordinate planning, documentation, development decisions and iterative reviews with the founding team.
