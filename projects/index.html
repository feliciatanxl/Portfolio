shieldquest / crime-prevention

Project SHIELD: ShieldQuest

Developing Project SHIELD (ShieldQuest), a youth-led interactive crime-prevention game for Delta Challenge 2026: Track B, organised in collaboration with the Singapore Police Force and National Crime Prevention Council. Designed as a mobile-first Progressive Web Application (PWA), ShieldQuest transforms traditional crime awareness into interactive decision-making and consequence-driven gameplay, encouraging responsible choices, peer intervention, and safer behaviours among youths.

>_ ShieldQuest | Interactive Crime Prevention
Developed a functional proof-of-concept featuring an interactive city-board, Delayed Consequence Engine, Peer SHIELD Mode, Guardian progression, and scenario management. Players navigate realistic situations, make decisions, and experience immediate and delayed consequences. The game aims to strengthen critical thinking, crime-prevention awareness, and peer intervention through experiential learning.
>_ SHIELD Framework | Behavioural Learning
Exploring a structured approach to crime-prevention education through scenario-based learning, interactive choices, and consequence simulation. Following stakeholder feedback, the team is refining the SHIELD framework, narrowing the gameplay towards physical crime prevention, and defining measurable behavioural outcomes to evaluate learning effectiveness and support future pilot implementation.
>_ NYC YCM | Conditional Grant Approval
Presented Project SHIELD at the Impact IRL Open Mic on 31 October 2026 under the National Youth Council's Young ChangeMakers (YCM) Grant. The project subsequently received conditional approval for funding of up to S$5,000, subject to refining its gameplay, physical crime-prevention scope, and measurable SHIELD framework outcomes. Full grant approval remains pending.
>_ Technology | Product Development
Building a responsive, mobile-first application using React, TypeScript, Node.js, and Progressive Web App technologies. Development focuses on interactive game mechanics, scenario management, consequence-driven interactions, responsive UI/UX, and scalable application architecture. The team is further refining the game experience and evaluation approach in preparation for potential pilot deployment.
